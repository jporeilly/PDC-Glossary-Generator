         .\pdc-remote.ps1 license

    3  Reload the lab data and re-register the data sources. The lab stack
       on 5433/9000 was NOT touched, but the catalog's record of it was.

    4  Reload the cast into Keycloak (users + PDC roles), then re-add any
       custom email domains to the css-auth-proxy allowlist. PDC rejects
       logins whose email domain is not on that list, and the list lived in
       a volume that has just been deleted.
